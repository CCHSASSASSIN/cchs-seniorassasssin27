    Rules and regulations

    Read every rule before you play. Signing up means you agreed to all of them. Rules marked Updated or New changed on or after November 18, 2026 (last update November 22).

    Assumption of risk, release, and parental consent (required)

    CCHS Senior Assassin is an informal, student-organized game. It is not sponsored, organized, or supervised by Cherry Creek High School or the Cherry Creek School District. By playing, I understand that the game involves risks, including slips, falls, collisions, traffic hazards, damage to property (including phones and clothing), and being mistaken for a threat by the public or police. I voluntarily accept these risks. To the fullest extent permitted by Colorado law, I release the student coordinators from claims for injury, loss, or damage arising from my participation, including claims based on the coordinators' ordinary negligence. I am responsible for my own actions and for any damage I cause, and I agree to follow all laws and any instruction from police or property owners.

    If I am under 18, my parent or guardian must also agree to these rules and this release on my behalf (as permitted by C.R.S. § 13-22-107) before I can play. Every player must agree individually; one partner cannot agree for the other.

    1. UpdatedAny and all water guns used to eliminate other players MUST be non realistic (IT MUST LOOK LIKE A WATER GUN: PLASTIC, COLORFUL AND BRIGHT, WITH LITTLE TO NO RESEMBLANCE TO A REAL FIREARM) to count. If you submit a video in which you eliminate another player with a realistic water gun, the elimination will be voided and you risk being put on the bounty board. (This is to prevent security issues.)

    2. UpdatedSchool property (THIS INCLUDES CCIC, PATHWAYS, AND UNIVERSITIES SUCH AS ACC, DU, AND CU DENVER) and school events (YES, INCLUDING THE PARKING LOTS) are off limits to get players out (school sanctioned event/school property = no senior assassin eliminations). Any players caught participating in the game on school property or at a school event will be INSTANTLY ELIMINATED.

    3. UpdatedYou may eliminate your target on their own home's property while they are OUTSIDE THE HOUSE: IN THE DRIVEWAY, ON THE LAWN, OR IN AN OPEN GARAGE. Eliminations made while the target is PHYSICALLY INSIDE THE HOUSE DO NOT COUNT. If a parent or guardian who lives there has told you that you cannot eliminate their family member at their home, ELIMINATIONS ON THAT PROPERTY DO NOT COUNT. IF ANYONE TELLS YOU TO LEAVE THEIR PROPERTY, YOU MUST LEAVE INSTANTLY. FAILURE TO DO SO = ELIMINATION.

    4. UpdatedIf you are attempting to eliminate a target within their vehicle, you are allowed to eliminate the target by opening their door if their door is unlocked. If their door is locked, you are not allowed to enter their car and eliminate them. The car must be parked with the engine off. Never open a car unless you are sure your target is inside, and never open, touch, or spray into a non-player's car. BY SIGNING UP FOR SENIOR ASSASSIN, YOU AGREE THAT ANY PLAYER HUNTING YOU HAS PERMISSION TO ELIMINATE YOU IN YOUR CAR IF IT IS UNLOCKED, OFF SCHOOL PROPERTY, AND PARKED WITH THE ENGINE OFF.

    5. Each player is assigned one target and receives their target's name via private message on Instagram. When you go to eliminate your target (or someone who is on the bounty board), you must take video evidence and send it to @cch.seniorassassin27 on Instagram. Please verify your target by sending their name when submitting footage. IF FOOTAGE IS UNCLEAR OR DOES NOT SHOW WHO WAS ELIMINATED, IT WILL NOT COUNT.

    6. Players are allowed to avoid elimination by wearing floaties (THESE FLOATIES MUST BE VISIBLE AND WORN ON THE ARM ABOVE THE ELBOW). If the floaties are hidden by clothing (this can be argued with the organizers) then you are out if eliminated. There will be "no-floaty" days throughout the course of the game. These will be Saturdays (starting Saturday, November 28) and some major holidays (THIS WILL BE EXPLICITLY HIGHLIGHTED ON THE CALENDAR). Floaties will not provide any protection from elimination on these days.

    7. UpdatedYou may wait for your target to leave their house ONLY FROM A PUBLIC SIDEWALK OR STREET. You may NOT WAIT ON YOUR TARGET'S PROPERTY (driveway, lawn, or garage). If you SEE your target outside on their own property (driveway, lawn, or open garage), you may go onto the property to eliminate them as allowed in rule 3. If a parent or guardian has told you that you cannot eliminate their family member at home, you may not go onto that property at all. If anyone tells you to leave, rule 3 applies: leave instantly or you are eliminated. Players cannot give permission for property they don't own. You may NOT enter neighbors' yards, hide in bushes or behind houses, or look into windows. Any legal disputes or conflicts between players that result from this ARE NOT THE RESPONSIBILITY OF CCHS SENIOR ASSASSIN.

    8. No eliminations may be attempted from the inside of a moving vehicle (no "drive-bys"). Any attempt to do so will result in immediate ejection from the games with no refund.

    9. UpdatedIf police or any authority ask you to stop or produce ID, comply immediately. The game does not override legal requirements. Put the water gun down, keep your hands visible, tell them you are playing a water gun game, and never run from police or security.

    10. Senior Assassin coordinators have final and complete authority over any disputes between players. There will be no arguing.

    11. UpdatedParticipants are not allowed to be eliminated when they are CLOCKED IN at a place of work, or attending a religious place of practice (such as a church, synagogue, mosque, temple, etc.). When they are clocked out (even if they are on break) and off property, they are open to elimination if they are not wearing floaties.

    12. UpdatedIf you are at a practice for a club sport (or other similar activities), you are still open to being eliminated. IF THE PRACTICE IS ON PRIVATE PROPERTY (SCHOOL, CLUB, FACILITY), YOU CANNOT ELIMINATE YOUR TARGET THERE WITHOUT THE OWNER'S PERMISSION. VIOLATORS WILL BE DISQUALIFIED. Your hunter can wait until you leave practice to eliminate you. You must wear FLOATIES in order to be safe from elimination (NO GOGGLES!). IF WEARING FLOATIES ABOVE YOUR ELBOWS HINDERS YOUR ABILITY TO PLAY A SPORT (OR GO TO THE GYM) YOU MAY WEAR THEM ELSEWHERE (OR TAPE THEM TO YOUR UPPER ARMS IF INHIBITED BY PADS OR GEAR).

    13. YOU MUST WEAR 2 FLOATIES. THEY DON'T HAVE TO BE INFLATED.

    14. UpdatedBOUNTY BOARD: every Sunday night at 11:59 PM during a round (the first check is the first Sunday at least a week after the round starts), any team that has not eliminated at least one player on its target team that week goes on the bounty board for the rest of the round. Anyone still in the game can eliminate a team on the bounty board, and it counts. Breaks count toward the week. FINAL CHECK: the days after a round's last Sunday check are checked when the round ends; teams that miss it start the next round on the bounty board for its first 7 days. NEW TARGETS: when your target team is fully eliminated (by you or anyone else), you inherit their target, the team they were hunting, for the rest of the round. Your new target shows up in My Account once the coordinators update it. If your target team is fully eliminated by someone else during a week, you are excused from that week's check.

    15. NewNo ski masks, face coverings, or full camouflage while playing. Hunters must be easy to recognize as students playing a game.

    16. NewWater only. Water guns may not be filled with anything else (no ice, paint, dye, soap, or other liquids). Never spray a non-player, a driver, or a moving vehicle.

    17. NewNo chasing or following anyone in a car, blocking roads or driveways, or speeding to reach a target. Any unsafe driving will result in immediate ejection from the game with no refund.

    18. NewUse the word "eliminate," not "kill" or "shoot," in posts, DMs, and group chats. Never post or send anything that could be read as a real threat.

    19. NewBy playing, you agree that elimination videos you appear in may be sent to and reviewed by the coordinators, and may be posted on @cch.seniorassassin27. Do not film or post bystanders or the inside of anyone's home. You may ask the coordinators to take down any video you appear in.

    20. NewCoordinators may remove any player for unsafe or illegal behavior. Coordinators may update these rules during the game; changes will be announced on @cch.seniorassassin27.

    21. NewPURGE DAYS: once a month there is a Purge Day. On a Purge Day (12:00 AM to 11:59 PM), anyone still in the game can eliminate ANY other player still in the game, not just their target (you can't eliminate your own partner). EVERY PURGE DAY IS ALSO A NO FLOATY DAY: floaties do not protect you. All other safe rules still apply (school property and events, clocked in at work, places of worship, inside your house, and rules 3 and 7). Purge eliminations count: the eliminator gets credit, and it counts as an elimination for that week's bounty board check. Targets do not change, except the normal inheriting when a team is fully eliminated. Send your video to @cch.seniorassassin27 with the name of who you eliminated, like any other elimination. PURGE DAYS: TUESDAY, DECEMBER 8; SATURDAY, JANUARY 16; WEDNESDAY, FEBRUARY 10; FRIDAY, MARCH 5. Later dates will be announced on the calendar. LOG YOUR PURGE ELIMINATION ON THE WEBSITE: log in, go to My Account, pick who you eliminated, tap the pink "Purge Day: I got them" button, then send that message and your video to @cch.seniorassassin27.
